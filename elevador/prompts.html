Texturas do Elevador

Uma imagem 2D por face do cabine. Geradas como albedo plano (sem sombra/brilho queimado) para as luzes do jogo continuarem mandando no clima — e com as zonas interativas em branco, para os botões, o display e o telefone seguirem em 3D por cima.

Frente · Portasdisplay acima fica em branco (renderizado em 3D)
1:1 · 2.6×2.7
Orthographic straight-on flat elevation, PBR base-color / albedo ONLY, of one interior surface of an old worn passenger elevator cabin. Even neutral lighting, NO baked shadows, NO highlights, NO hotspots, NO vignette (lighting is added later in-engine). Materials: dark aged brushed stainless steel, gunmetal, tungsten-grime patina, satin scuffed finish, faint vertical water streaks, fingerprint smudges, micro-scratches, rivet rows, recessed panel seams, tarnished brass accents. Muted desaturated palette: charcoal and gun-grey. No perspective, no lens distortion, squared flush edges, seamless flat, 4K tileable texture, eerie but neutral exposure. NEGATIVE: people, hands, readable text, warped geometry, cast shadows, colored light, room reflections, fisheye, watermark, logo, rendered buttons, glowing screens. SUBJECT: the front wall, dominated by a pair of closed two-leaf sliding steel elevator doors with a central vertical seam, a recessed rubber safety edge, a low scuffed kick-plate and a thin squared door frame / architrave. Above the doors, an EMPTY dark recessed rectangular housing (blank, no digits) reserved for a floor indicator. NO buttons on this wall.

Por que "em branco"? deixando o painel, o display e o telefone vazios na imagem, eu renderizo esses elementos em 3D por cima — assim eles continuam funcionando e eu posso colocar mais andares (descida mais longa, código maior, paradas no meio do caminho) sem regerar nenhuma arte.
Proporção = largura×altura reais da face, pra pedir o aspecto certo no gerador.
